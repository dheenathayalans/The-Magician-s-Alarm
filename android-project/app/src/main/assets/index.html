SPIRIT’S POSITIVITY CHALLENGE

By GD • The Sacred Purpose of This App

"Our job as sadhaks is to deny reality to negativity and doom in this moment."
🌌 1. The Call & The Collective Shift

GD: Currently, there’s a tired, hopeless ‘whatever’ feeling in the collective—Nothing I do is going to make any difference so what’s the point? Hamara kuch nahin ho sakta. Sab khatam ho gaya.

The earth’s greatest glory is yet to come but a section of the media, especially social media, is presenting every shift as proof of end-times. Another part of social media is waiting for some savior: a solar flash or new technology or alien intervention. One side says nothing can be done, the other says someone else will do it for us. Both are doing the same work of making us feel bleak and hopeless.

Our job as sadhaks is to align and agree with neither side.
Our job is to deny reality to negativity and doom in this moment.

The collective darkness is heavy right now so it feels like it's harder to get out of it at times. But the contrast, whenever you do step out, is equally fantastic. When you rise out of it, you instantly feel light and happy.

Anytime I get any negative thought these days, Spirit pauses me and says "Okay, now is the time to go out and do something positive for five minutes." Then I go out to the balcony and don’t come back inside for five minutes, until the rampage of positivity is done.

🧙‍♂️ 2. The 5x5 Practice & The Magician's Law

The practice Spirit is suggesting for all of us now is to be actively positive five times a day for five minutes each.

That’s less than half an hour in a day. Whenever you get stuck in any problem—with your family, your neighbour, on the street—remember you have five to complete. Instead of going into panic or depression, argument or analysis, go straight into prayer, blessing and manifestation.

If you complete five every day, you will have done 150 prayers and blessings in a month, and you will see the miracles it brings into your life. It’s so simple: 30 minutes a day for 30 days. No blaming God, no blaming spirituality, no blaming the world—

"You are the magician. You will either do the magic, or you will sit and cry. If you open up as a magician, you will see things changing around you. With 150 prayers and blessings, it’s impossible that things don’t change. It’s impossible."

Why be afraid of the future when the power to create it is with us? We don’t use the power and then we blame God.

✨ 3. The 10 Sacred Superpower Tools

If you are wondering what to do in those five minutes, here are ten options:

1. New Ho’oponopono
"I LOVE you. I SEE you. I HONOR you. I BLESS you." Say these lines with feeling for different people who are in your field of Consciousness. If you are upset with someone, say it three times for that person. You can say this for your house, your business and your child’s school too.
2. Prayer (The Double Request)
Communicate from the heart to God, Spirit, Babaji, Jesus, Mother Mary, archangels and guides and ask for help in any matter. The highest form of prayer asks for God. It’s a double request: The first part says "I am ready for God, please come." The second part says that "if there’s anything blocking this, please let me see it."
3. Forgiveness (Dual-Forgiveness)
Spirit keeps reminding us: Grievance is the only thing that keeps you away from Heaven. The desire to be angry at God takes away this ever-present Heaven. Forgiveness restores us to Heaven. So if you feel like you are unable to bless or pray for someone, go straight to dual-forgiveness. For example: "I forgive you for being so scared and confused. I forgive me for being so scared and confused."
4. Gratitude
Appreciate what you already have in your life in this moment: a roof over your head, food, family, clothes, money in the bank. That’s better than many on the planet right now. Be grateful for that.
5. ‘Thank you God for…’ (Advance Manifestation)
Thanking God in advance is a manifestation technique to frame your problem as if it’s already solved, even though in ‘reality’ it’s not occurred already. For example: "Thank you God in advance for fixing my knees. Thank you God in advance for making this house situation so easy for me."
6. ‘Wouldn’t it be nice if…’
Instead of pushing others around you and the world to change, gently speak what you wish to see. For example: "Wouldn’t it be nice if my son could love himself instead of feeling guilty? Wouldn’t it be nice if my mother could forgive my father?"
7. Affirmations
Speaking affirmation with quietness and confidence is very powerful. If the mind is in a fighting mood, don’t try to outshout the negativity with your positivity. That’s missing the point. If the resistance is too strong and you can’t bring yourself to think of any affirmations, begin by listening to affirmations for five minutes. Even that will make you feel better.
8. Handing It Over
If nothing makes sense, call Spirit in. Surrender the situation. Don’t struggle to solve problems from a split mind which is confused and guilty. Be willing to see the problem—and you as you perceive yourself—are both illusions. Ask Spirit to dissolve all your errors of perception and to restore perfect peace.
9. Blessing
Speak blessings for individuals, for the world, even for AI. Whatever you are worried about in the world, you can bless. For example: "May you be happy. May you experience the peace of God."
10. Declaring the Highest Truth
At the end of every session, always declare the highest truth for others. Otherwise we sometimes see them as victims even while we are blessing them or praying for them. So we can start with blessings but end with the highest declarations:
• I am the Light. You are the Light.
• I am complete, You are complete.
• I am a beautiful creation of God. You are a beautiful creation of God.
• I deserve perfect happiness. You deserve perfect happiness.
• I am one with God. You are one with God.
• So you don’t actually need anything—we are just dreaming a dream.
• May they recognise their oneness and completion and be happy in every situation.
🕯️ 4. Sustaining the Power & Being the Lighthouse

These ten tools should be on hand through the day. The moment you feel a negative feeling coming on, catch one of them. See whichever one helps. You can do just one of these for the full five-minute session, or different combinations, whatever works for you.

How do you know it’s working? If it’s fake mental positivity or bypassing, you won’t feel it. If the positive rampage is done properly, you get a feeling of the future in that moment. You will feel the joy of it.

We have all the knowledge. Now we have to put it into practice. Who’s going to do it? Only you. We are magicians. But if the magician gets depressed and lazy and stops using his gifts, not only does he drown, he takes others down with him—and if he’s really clever, he tells the others that he’s drowning because of them!

It’s not the situation that’s making us helpless—not using our gifts is what makes us helpless. So use these superpowers daily. If you don’t finish five during the day then in the night, before sleeping, finish the five. If you have a problem, apply it there first. If you have a smooth day, send blessings and prayers for others who are struggling around you: for your family, for your house, for your children and your staff.

Stretch each session for five minutes. Sometimes we finish it too quickly. The power is in sustaining. If you can sustain a thought for around five minutes, it gets manifested. It’s an investment of half an hour a day. And if we can't give that much to God, how can we blame God and say spirituality failed? You failed yourself. You didn’t follow the basics. Being grateful for each day. Blessing others during the day. Forgiving others before sleep.

The main function of all these is to interrupt the negative stream. Spirit says this is the responsibility of the seeker at this time. Otherwise, once the negativity starts, it goes on for hours. When the dark mood takes over, it feels easy and natural to slide with it. Changing direction takes a firm decision. But in five minutes, you can completely switch situations in your life and in your body. If we are in the right space, we can immediately shift the problem before the frequency of the problem settles down.

The strange aches and pain we feel in our bodies are these frequencies which have settled down. We agreed with them over months and years. Slowly, slowly they began settling in the body. And now it confirms itself. It’s like you allowed a loudspeaker inside your house and now the loudspeaker is going to keep asserting ‘It’s all useless! It’s all hopeless!’

To step out of the collective gloom needs guts, the right tools and practise in handling this. A newcomer who has never experienced this shift will flounder. He will be carried away by the collective mind. We have been healing, clearing, meditating; it’s like we have been going to the gym everyday, and these muscles have developed. Now is the time to be diligent about it. Keep reminders on your phone for whatever times in the day work for you if required. Keep a timer for five minutes so you don’t stop after a few seconds.

"Every time we do it, it becomes a little easier. When we start seeing the miracles in our life, we get more confidence. The highest miracle to ask for is that we can be in this miracle space 24/7. We want to expand the five minutes to the entire day. We were meant to be the lighthouse in this darkness."
— GD

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

The Magician's Alarm ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

1. 100% Local-First Data Storage

The Magician's Alarm operates strictly on a local-first architecture. All user data, including daily practice logs, completed session counts, alarm times, reminder sounds, streak metrics, and unlocked fortune cookie rewards, are stored exclusively on your local device via local storage.

2. No Data Collection or Tracking

We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers. There are no third-party tracking scripts, cloud user accounts, or advertising SDKs included in the app.

3. Device Permissions

The app may request the following standard Android permissions:

  • Notifications & Alarms: To alert you at your chosen 5 daily practice times and bedtime queue.
  • Vibration / Audio: To play your selected soothing chimes and bell sounds when alarms fire.

4. Data Backup & User Control

You have full control over your data. You can export your session history as a JSON file or import a previous backup at any time from within the app settings.

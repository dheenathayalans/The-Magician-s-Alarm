Privacy Policy for The Magician's Alarm

Effective Date: October 3, 2026
Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Thank you for using The Magician's Alarm: Spirit's Positivity Challenge ("the App"). Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled.

1. No Personal Data Collection

The Magician's Alarm does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.

  • No account registration, email address, name, or phone number is required.
  • We do not collect location data, IP addresses, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use any third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or external behavioral analytics.

2. Local Device Storage (Offline First)

All application data—including your scheduled alarm times, session streaks, completed daily challenge counts, and collected fortune cookie messages—is stored exclusively on your personal device using standard local storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences and HTML5 LocalStorage). This data never leaves your device and is erased if you uninstall the App.

3. Android Permissions & Usage

The App requests standard Android device permissions strictly for local clock and alarm functionality:

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM: Used solely by Android's native AlarmManager to trigger your 5 daily positivity reminders at the exact minute scheduled.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver high-priority alarm notification banners to wake your device screen.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Temporarily keeps the screen active for the duration of the 5-minute positivity practice.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Appear on Top): Allows the gentle practice ceremony to be displayed when your device is locked without requiring manual unlocking.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is suitable for general audiences of all ages seeking peace, mindfulness, and positivity.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised policy will be posted on this webpage with an updated effective date.
